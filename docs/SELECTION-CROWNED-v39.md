# Selection panel and Crowned awakenings v39

Normal/Shiny collection-grid dropdowns are removed. Choose a Pokémon card, then use the right-hand detail panel's Normal/Shiny buttons immediately above the actual partner selection button. The preview, selected form, partner button and saved team choice stay synchronized; unowned shiny options remain locked. Team-management color controls are retained.

Zacian and Zamazenta now have their own Crowned-form transformation presentation, separate from Mega and Primal effects. Cyan-white or pink-gold flames wrap the wolf and gather into a turbulent, swirling light cocoon. At 2.05 seconds the Crowned sprite appears with its cry as the flame sheets peel away; sparks and local bloom fade by 4.1 seconds. Uses existing normal/shiny form sprites, native relic requirements, stage unlock and native form duration. It does not trigger the Mega Tera theme.

Early switches, fainting and reversion clean up the effect. Returning to a benched Crowned Pokémon restores its form without replaying the animation or cry. Cries follow the native sound-effect volume and mute setting.

Visual reference: the official Pokémon Evolutions episode The Champion, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v56BRim3g0w (awakening sequence about 4:20–4:44). These are original in-game pixel effects, not imported video frames. Audio/source provenance is in SOURCE_LICENSES/crowned-v39.

The v39 update is cumulative over v37 and also works over v38. Merge its folders into the game root beside index.html, replace matching files and refresh the page. It includes the earlier v38 fixes and Karma removal. Live network co-op was not tested.

V40: Bloom radius and brightness pulse while three counter-rotating wisps swirl around the core. Review buttons now play the whole 4.1-second sequence instead of freezing its clock.
