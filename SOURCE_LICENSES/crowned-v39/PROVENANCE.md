# Crowned transformation reference and audio

The animation is original canvas/pixel VFX, adapting the visual structure observed in the official Pokémon Evolutions Episode 1, The Champion (The Official Pokémon YouTube channel): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v56BRim3g0w — approximately 4:20–4:44. The heroes gather colored light; Zacian uses cyan/white and Zamazenta uses pink/gold; glowing forms reveal their armor. No footage, background, music or animation frames from that video are bundled.

Official designs and form context: https://swordshield.pokemon.com/en-us/pokemon-galar-region/zacian/ and https://swordshield.pokemon.com/en-us/pokemon-galar-region/zamazenta/ . Existing game sprites and form obtainment are retained.

Species cries copied unchanged from PokeAPI/cries main, cries/pokemon/latest/888.ogg and 889.ogg: https://github.com/PokeAPI/cries . These are Pokémon audio assets, with underlying rights held by the Pokémon rights holders, used by this noncommercial fan project.

User-provided zaciangif.gif and transformnation.gif were inspected as motion references for wrapping flames, swirling cocoons and the luminous reveal. Their frames are not bundled or used as game sprites.

V41 Behemoth Blade leap/charge/cleave motion also references user-provided 3.gif. No video or GIF frames are bundled. The attack effect is original procedural pixel VFX using existing animated normal/shiny Zacian sprites.
