Credits

Pokémon Tower Defense is a free, non-commercial fan game made by one person. It has no ads, no paid items and takes no donations. It's built on the work of the artists and projects below. Thank you all.

Pokémon sprites

The animated Pokémon sprites come from PMDCollab SpriteCollab (GitHub), drawn by the PMDCollab community and based on the style of Pokémon Mystery Dungeon (Spike Chunsoft). They're used under the CC BY-NC 4.0 license. Each sprite's artists are listed on its page in the SpriteCollab viewer and in the credits.txt file next to it in the game's files. The promo Pokémon are those same sprites with an accessory drawn in, shared under the same license.

The Gym badge pictures come from PokeAPI's sprite collection; the badges themselves are Nintendo / GAME FREAK's.

Maps and tiles

The Kanto maps are rebuilt from the map data of Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen (Nintendo / GAME FREAK), decoded with the help of the pret pokefirered project.

Extra Gen 3-style tilesets come from Team Aqua's Asset Repo, free to use with credit for non-commercial projects. Artists:

  • Tileset artists and rippers: Ekat (Ekat99), Vurtax, Heartlessdragoon, TheDeadHeroAlistair, Zein, Skidmarc25, fisham33, Anonalpaca, Idilio, Slimshady, Amethyst, Crim, Smeargletail, princess-phoenix (Pokémon Reborn), Hek-el-grande, Kalarie (Orange Islands tiles), leob0505, FM, Zeikaro, KyuZee, Oomer, yoshord, and J-Treecko252 (the 3D trees and grass, from Tree Tiles Ver. 10 and Animated Grass Tiles Ver. 01).
  • Assembly, reformatting and submission: Rahtak, Yumekua, Kumatora and ShinyDragonHunter.
  • Plus the many more artists named in each tileset's own credits file, and Pokémon Essentials.

Item icons

Held item icons come from the pokemon-assets package (MIT license). The item artwork itself belongs to Nintendo / GAME FREAK.

Code and fonts

  • three.js (MIT) draws the battlefield.
  • Supabase (supabase-js, MIT) runs accounts, saves and chat.
  • The tower defense engine is inspired by the Bloons Tower Defense series (Ninja Kiwi).
  • Fonts: Fredoka and Pixelify Sans (SIL Open Font License), from Google Fonts.
  • Sound effects and music are generated in the game's own code.
